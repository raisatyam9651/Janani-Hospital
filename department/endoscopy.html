FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about endoscopic procedures and diagnostic services

Preparation varies by procedure type. For upper endoscopy, fast for 8-12 hours. For colonoscopy, follow specific bowel preparation instructions. Our team will provide detailed preparation guidelines.
Most patients receive conscious sedation to ensure comfort during procedures. Our anesthesia team monitors you throughout to ensure safety and comfort.
Upper endoscopy typically takes 15-30 minutes, while colonoscopy takes 30-60 minutes. Recovery time is usually 1-2 hours depending on sedation used.
Preliminary results are available immediately after the procedure. If biopsies are taken, pathology results are typically available within 3-5 business days.
