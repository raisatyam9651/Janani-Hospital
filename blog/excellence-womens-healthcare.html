About Janani Hospital

Janani Multispeciality Hospital and Research Centre in Vijayapura is a premier healthcare institution dedicated to providing comprehensive and compassionate medical services. With state-of-the-art facilities, highly experienced doctors, and 24/7 emergency care, we have established ourselves as a leader in healthcare excellence in the region.

Our specialties range from advanced Women's Healthcare, Obstetrics and Gynecology (OBG), IVF and Fertility treatments, to complex General Surgery, Pediatrics, Urology, and Orthopedics. Every day, we work tirelessly to ensure that our patients receive world-class care in a safe and supportive environment.

This recognition motivates us to further elevate our standards and continue our mission of healing and transforming lives. We thank our patients, staff, and the community for their unwavering trust and support.
